Investors judge a demo by its reliability. A 60-second "cold start" wait on a free tier signals a hobbyist project.

*   **Platform:** Use **Railway (Hobby)** or **Cloud Run** with `min-instances: 1`. The app must be **instant** when the investor clicks the link in your email.
*   **The "Exclusionary" UI:** Add a simple "Access Code" gate to the Streamlit UI. 
    *   *The Pitch:* "I am granting you a window into the private reasoning terminal." This shifts the power dynamic from "please look at my project" to "I am granting you credentials for a technical audit."
